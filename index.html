FAQ

Good to know.

Where exactly is Godrej Barça Phase 2?

At Godrej MSR City, Yerthiganahalli, Shettigere, North Bengaluru 562157 — about 15 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.

What is the price of Godrej MSR City Barça Phase 2?

2 BHK homes start from ₹1.59 Cr* and 3 BHK homes go up to about ₹2.99 Cr*, across five layouts from ~1,200 to ~2,275 sq ft. Prices are indicative; ask us for the latest cost sheet.

What is the EOI and how much is it?

The Expression of Interest (EOI) of ₹1 Lakh puts you on the Phase 2 priority list, so you can select your unit before general sales.

How did Phase 1 of Godrej Barça do?

Godrej Properties reported selling about 1,450 homes worth ₹2,000 crore at the launch of the first phase of Godrej MSR City (Godrej Barça) in June 2025.

When is possession?

Possession follows the timeline registered with Karnataka RERA for Phase 2. We share the exact date with your cost sheet.

How big is the township?

Godrej MSR City spans 62 acres. Phase 2 of Godrej Barça has 805 residences, with a 1.5 lakh sq ft clubhouse, lagoon, aqua park and a 1-acre forest park.

I'm an NRI — can I see the home before booking?

Yes. We schedule a live video tour at your convenience, walk you through every room, then guide you through the EOI digitally.

Can you arrange a site visit?

Yes — weekday or weekend. Share a time and we plan your visit to the sales gallery and model home.
