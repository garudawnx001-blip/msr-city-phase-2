FAQ

Good to know.

Where exactly is Godrej Barça Phase 2?

At MSR City, Shettigere, North Bengaluru — about 15 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.

What configurations and prices are available?

2 BHK (~1,200 sq ft) from ₹1.59 Cr*, 2 BHK Luxe (~1,350 sq ft) from ₹1.79 Cr*, Premium 3 BHK + 2T (~1,750 sq ft) from ₹2.32 Cr*, Grand 3 BHK (~1,930 sq ft) from ₹2.56 Cr* and 3 BHK Luxe (~2,275 sq ft) from ₹2.99 Cr*. Prices are indicative.

What is the EOI and how much is it?

The Expression of Interest (EOI) reserves your priority for Phase 2. The EOI amount is ₹1 Lakh and gives you priority unit selection.

How big is the township?

Godrej Barça is part of a 62-acre township with a 1.5 lakh sq ft Balinese clubhouse, aqua park, lagoon pool, a 1-acre forest park and courts for more than a dozen sports.

I'm an NRI — can I see the home before booking?

Yes. Book a live video tour, scheduled at your convenience. We walk you through the model home and explain every step of the EOI.
