FAQ

Good to know.

Where exactly is Godrej Barça Phase 2?

At Godrej MSR City, Yerthiganahalli, Shettigere, North Bengaluru 562157 — about 15 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.

What configurations and prices are available?

2 BHK homes from ₹1.59 Cr* and 3 BHK homes up to about ₹2.99 Cr*, across five layouts from ~1,200 to ~2,275 sq ft. Prices are indicative; ask us for the latest cost sheet.

What is the EOI and how much is it?

The Expression of Interest (EOI) of ₹1 Lakh puts you on the Phase 2 priority list, so you get to select your unit before general sales.

When is possession?

Possession follows the timeline registered with Karnataka RERA for Phase 2. We share the exact date with your cost sheet.

How big is the township?

Godrej MSR City spans 62 acres. Phase 2 of Godrej Barça has 805 residences, with a 1.5 lakh sq ft clubhouse, lagoon, aqua park and a 1-acre forest park.

I'm an NRI — can I see the home before booking?

Yes. We schedule a live video tour at your convenience and walk you through every room, then guide you through the EOI digitally.

Can you arrange a site visit?

Yes — weekday or weekend. Share a time and we plan the visit to the sales gallery and model home.
