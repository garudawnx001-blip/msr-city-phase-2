Godrej's project in Shettigere

When people search for Godrej Shettigere they mean Godrej MSR City — Godrej Properties' 62-acre integrated township at Shettigere / Yerthiganahalli, off Bagalur and Airport Road in North Bengaluru (PIN 562157). The residential neighbourhood here is Godrej Barça, inspired by Barcelona's courtyards, colours and Gaudí-style mosaics.

Where exactly is Shettigere?

Shettigere sits north-east of Yelahanka and south of Devanahalli, between Bagalur Road and the Airport Road corridor. It is about 15 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport, close to KIADB Aerospace Park, IFCI Financial City and schools such as DPS Bangalore North.
