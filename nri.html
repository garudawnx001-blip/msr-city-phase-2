Can NRIs buy at Godrej Barça?

Yes. Non-resident Indians can buy residential property in India. We guide you through the Phase 2 EOI, unit selection and the paperwork from wherever you live.

How the live video tour works

Pick a time that suits your time zone. Our advisor walks you through the model home room by room on a video call, answers questions live and shares the cost sheet and floor plans right after.

Steps to book from abroad

  • Send us your preferred layout and budget.
  • Join a live video tour of the model home.
  • Submit the ₹1 Lakh EOI to join the Phase 2 priority list.
  • Select your unit before general sales; we help with the documents that follow.

Why Godrej MSR City appeals to NRIs

A 62-acre township by Godrej Properties, about 15 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport — easy for visits home — with resort-style amenities and Barcelona-inspired design. Phase 1 saw about 1,450 homes sold at launch.
