2 BHK floor plans

The 2 BHK Classic (about 1,200 sq ft) and 2 BHK Luxe (about 1,350 sq ft) both have two bedrooms and two bathrooms, an open living and dining area and a separate kitchen. The Luxe layout adds a larger living room and more generous bedrooms.

3 BHK floor plans

Three 3 BHK layouts cover growing families: the 3 BHK Premium (about 1,750 sq ft, two bathrooms), the 3 BHK Grand (about 1,930 sq ft, three bathrooms) and the flagship 3 BHK Luxe (about 2,275 sq ft, three bathrooms).

How the master plan is laid out

Six residential buildings line a central landscaped boulevard. Pools, the lagoon and the clubhouse sit at the heart of the township, with a forest park and future development parcels around them.
