Where is Godrej MSR City?

Godrej MSR City is at Yerthiganahalli, Shettigere, in North Bengaluru (PIN 562157). It sits on the airport side of the city, about 15 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.

Why North Bengaluru

The area around the airport is one of Bengaluru's fastest-growing corridors, with the KIADB Aerospace Park, IFCI Financial City and a growing cluster of offices, universities and hospitals nearby.

Nearby places

  • Travel: Kempegowda International Airport, Airport City Metro Station
  • Work: IFCI Financial City, KIADB Aerospace Park
  • Education: Delhi Public School Bangalore North, Reva University
  • Health & shopping: Relive Hospital Bagalur, Prestige 13 North Mall
